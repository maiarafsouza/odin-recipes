Brazilian Carrot Cake

A cake so good it's a national symbol.

This cake surpasses american carrot cakes in both ease of technique and flavor. The carrots are blended in the batter instead of shredded, there are no nuts, raisins or spices to steal the show and the brigadeiro is just the perfect compliment to the carrot flavor.

Originally at: Ash Baber

Ingredients

For the cake:

  • 300g carrots, peeled and slightly chopped
  • 250g granulated sugar
  • 250ml vegetable oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 250g plain flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

For the brigadeiro:

  • 1 can condensed milk (397g)
  • 150g dark chocolate
  • 150ml double cream
  • 50g unsalted butter
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Start by pre-heating your oven to 180c/350f. Also grease and line an 8 inch cake tin, leave this aside.
  2. In a blender, add the carrots, sugar, oil, eggs and vanilla extract. Blend this until the mixture is smooth.
  3. In a large bowl, add the flour, baking powder and salt. Whisk these together.
  4. Now add the blended carrot mixture to your flour mixture. Whisk just until you can no longer see any flour.
  5. Pour this into your prepared cake tin and bake at 180c/350f for around 1 hour - 1 hour 10 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the centre comes out clean. If at any point during baking you feel like the top of the cake is turning too brown, you can cover the top with some tinfoil.
  6. Once baked, leave to cool down.
  7. Making the brigadeiro:
    1. Once the cake has cooled down, you can make the brigadeiro.
    2. In a pot, add all the ingredients. Place this pot onto medium heat.
    3. Keep heating this, whiles stirring, until the chocolate and butter has melted.
    4. Once it has melted, carry on heating it and stirring at the same time, until the mixture thickens slightly. You want it to be thick, but still pourable.
    5. Take your cake out of the tin and place this onto a wire rack, upside down. Then place your wire rack on top of a tray.
    6. Pour the brigadeiro over this, until the whole cake is covered. Use a spoon or palette knife to smooth out the top.
    7. Let this sit for about 10 minutes, then move the cake onto a plate or board.
    8. Now you can cut into it and enjoy!
